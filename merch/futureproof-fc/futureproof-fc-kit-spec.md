# Futureproof FC — Kit Build Specification (v1)

**Status:** internal creative brief / draft build spec — concepts, not final artwork
**Date:** 2026-07-22
**Owner:** Kris (KK) — creative direction and final approval
**Purpose:** turn the five merch "concept credits" from the World Cup design hackathon
into a manufacturer-ready capsule of Futureproof-branded football kits, in the
festival's own colours and visual language, timed to World Cup 2026 energy and
wearable at the festival (Oct 28–30, 2026).
**Companion visual:** `futureproof-fc-kit-board.html` (self-contained; open in a
browser to see the jerseys, colourways, and placement callouts).

> This is a design brief, not approved public copy. Nothing here is a public
> claim, a confirmed product, or an announcement. Palette values are pulled from
> the live site system (`tailwind.config.ts`) so the merch and the website read
> as one brand.

---

## 0. Keep this lane clean (IP)

Ride the World Cup **energy**, never the trademarks. This is **our own
federation** — original crest, our marks, "Futureproof FC." Do **not** use FIFA
or official tournament emblems, the official trophy, or host-city official
logos/wordmarks. National-team *kit codes* (crest + sponsor + number, sleeve
patches, namesets) are a genre, not protected IP — emulating the genre with our
own artwork is clean to make and clean to sell.

---

## 1. The organizing idea — "Futureproof FC"

Treat the festival like a national side for one tournament summer. A kit is only
ever three things, and on ours they all say the same thing — *you were in the
room*:

| Kit element | Our translation |
|---|---|
| **Crest** (club badge) | The **raven-and-crystal** in a shield/roundel — "EST. 2026 · VANCOUVER" |
| **Sponsor** (centre-chest billboard) | The `FUTUREPROOF` dot-matrix wordmark |
| **Number** | **No. 26** (the year) by default; personalized name + number for buyers |

Everything downstream — colourways, patches, tags — hangs off that one idea, so
the five kits read as a coherent range rather than five unrelated shirts.

## 2. Colour system

Palette is lifted directly from the site tokens. **Hex is authoritative.** Match
methods differ by technique:

- **Sublimated graphics (match kit):** colour-match to the RGB/hex in the print
  file. No Pantone needed for the all-over art.
- **Embroidery thread:** pick Madeira/Isacord thread numbers at sampling against
  a printed swatch of the hex.
- **Dyed trims + knit socks/collars:** confirm **PANTONE TCX** by physical
  lab-dip. Numbers below are a *family direction only* — do not treat as final.

| Token | Hex | Role on the kits | Pantone TCX direction (confirm by lab-dip) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Salmon | `#d2645d` | Home hero colour; primary accent | coral / terracotta family |
| Ink | `#0A0A0B` | Ground, blackout third, trims | near-black |
| Aurora (bright) | `#5EEAD4` | Signal, collar trims, taping | bright aqua |
| Aurora (mid) | `#14B8A6` | Away ribbons, sleeve patch | teal |
| Parchment | `#F5F1E8` | Away ground, editorial relief | warm cream |
| Mountain violet | `#8B5CF6` | Third-kit iridescence, dome | violet |
| Starfield night | `#172231` | Special-edition ground | deep navy |
| Starfield teal | `#61938c` | Special-edition constellation | muted sea-green |
| BC + AI lime | `#daf142` | Keeper kit; single-stitch signal elsewhere | electric chartreuse |

**Palette discipline:** no single colour family runs the whole capsule (per the
brand guide — Futureproof is not all-teal / all-lime / all-Halloween-orange).
Lime is deliberately reserved for the goalkeeper kit and single accent stitches
so it stays electric.

## 4. The range — five kits

Hexes per kit. "Front" = sublimation/print direction; "Back" = nameset + any

## 5. All the bits & bobs (finishing checklist)

This is the difference between "a nice tee" and "a kit." Pull any per garment.

- [ ] Embroidered **crest** — raven & crystal (chain-stitch or bullion wire)
- [ ] **Maker's mark** — small Futureproof dot, right chest
- [ ] **Centre wordmark** — `FUTUREPROOF` dot-matrix (sublimation or twill)
- [ ] **Sleeve patches** — edition date + Vancouver roundel (+ optional track badge)
- [ ] Custom **nameset** — condensed numerals + name (twill / sublimated / heat)
- [ ] **Woven collar loop tag** — raised, at the nape
- [ ] **Back-neck taping** — printed manifesto line inside the collar
- [ ] **Story / jock tag** — satin woven, design meaning + edition number
- [ ] **Hem flag tab** — side-seam woven tab
- [ ] **Content & care label** — recycled/organic content, worth reading
- [ ] **Match-detail embroidery** — `OCT 28–30 2026 · VANCOUVER` inside hem (optional)
- [ ] **Signature stitch** — tiny KK / Futureproof autograph (optional)

**Material rule:** no PVC / rubber patches anywhere. Woven, embroidered,
chain-stitch, or (for a premium DIY hit) leather/cork/deadstock-fabric patches only.

## 6. The material fork (decide early — it drives the whole tech pack)

### Option A — The Match Kit
- **Fabric:** recycled polyester performance knit (rPET, target GRS-certified),
  interlock or micro-pique, ~140–160 gsm.
- **Decoration:** full edge-to-edge **sublimation** — any colourway, no print-count
  limit. Crest/nameset applied as embroidery/twill after print.
- **Story:** honest eco line — *made from plastic already in the world, not new.*
- **Best for:** Home salmon fade, the iridescent Third, the Keeper, the dome burst.
- **Note:** this is what the manufacturing tool is built for.

### Option B — The Terrace Shirt
- **Fabric:** heavyweight **organic cotton** jersey (~220–240 gsm), or cotton
  pique for a polo collar.
- **Decoration:** water-based/discharge **screenprint** + real **embroidery** +
  **woven patches**. Graphics live where print/stitch go (chest, back, sleeves),
  not edge-to-edge.
- **Story:** the "no plastic garbage" instinct, literally — natural fibre, natural hand.
- **Best for:** everyday wear, the cotton crowd, the campfire counterpart.

**Recommendation (current working assumption): do both**, as a two-tier line —
the sublimated **match kit** as the drop moment, the cotton **terrace shirt** as
the everyday flex. Public square and campfire, in poly and cotton. Start the tech
pack with whichever KK would wear first.

## 7. Nameset system

Real kits ship a hero number and *also* support personalization — do both:

- **Default:** `No. 26` (the festival year). Keeper kit is `No. 1`.
- **Personalized:** buyer name (caps, arched) + chosen number, made-to-order.
- **Number font direction:** draw a custom numeral set from the **dot-matrix
  wordmark** (ties the nameset to the identity) *or* an editorial Cormorant-derived
  serif numeral for a luxury/vanity read. The number font is half a kit's
  identity — worth a dedicated sampling pass.

## 9. Beyond the pitch (rest of the merch brainstorm)

Held to KK's rule — human, DIY, no plastic garbage. Parked here so it isn't lost;
deeper dive on request.

- Screen-printed **bandannas** (salmon-run repeat / manifesto grid)
- Strike-anywhere **matches** (printed box) + a refillable **brass lighter**,
  engraved raven-and-crystal — "carry fire, build what lasts"
- Enamel **pins** (raven, salmon, dome) — collectible/tradeable
- Chain-stitch sew-on **patches** (DIY patch culture)
- Riso / letterpress **field-notes zine** + manifesto posters
- Enamel **camp mug** or a **cedar** object — no-plastic, PNW-native
