The premise: our own federation

A kit is only three things — a crest, a sponsor, and a number — and on ours they all mean the same thing: you were in the room. The raven-and-crystal becomes the club badge. FUTUREPROOF takes the sponsor block. The number is the year, or yours. We treat the festival like a national side for one glorious tournament summer, then wear it to the festival in October.

Everything downstream — colourways, patches, tags — hangs off that one idea, so the five kits read as a range, not five random shirts.

The five slots

Home · Away · Third · Keeper · Special

The classic kit range maps cleanly onto your five concept credits — a signature home, a wearable away, a blacked-out third flex, the loud goalkeeper, and a numbered Space-Centre collector. Make all five, or pick the two or three you'll actually run first.

One lane to keep clean: this is our federation — original crest, our marks, “Futureproof FC.” We ride the World Cup energy, never FIFA / official tournament emblems or host-city logos. Safe to make, safe to sell.

Two ways to make it

The one real fork — and it's a values question as much as a materials one. It changes the whole tech pack, so worth deciding early.

Option A · The match kit

Recycled performance knit

rPET (recovered-plastic) performance fabric, full edge-to-edge sublimation. Every graphic above can run anywhere on the shirt. The honest eco story: made from plastic already in the world, not new. True on-pitch feel; best for the salmon fade, the iridescent raven, the keeper, and the dome burst.

  • All-over print, any colourway, no print-count limits
  • Moisture-wicking, lightweight, “real football shirt”
  • What the manufacturing tool is built for
Option B · The terrace shirt

Heavyweight organic cotton

Your “no plastic garbage” instinct, literally. Natural-fibre body, graphics placed where screenprint + real embroidery + woven patches go — chest, back, sleeves — rather than edge-to-edge. Wears like a premium tee or rugby shirt off the pitch. The campfire to the match kit's public square.

  • Screenprint the salmon run / manifesto; embroider the crest
  • Woven & chain-stitch patches, no PVC anywhere
  • Everyday-wearable, natural hand-feel

My rec: do both, as a two-tier line — the sublimated match kit as the drop moment, the cotton terrace shirt as the everyday flex. Public square and campfire, in poly and cotton. Start the tech pack with whichever one you'd wear first.
