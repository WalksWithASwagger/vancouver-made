## The concept in 30 seconds

**Futureproof FC** — treat the festival like a national side for the World Cup
summer, then wear it at the festival (Oct 28–30, 2026). A kit is only ever three
things, and ours all say *you were in the room*:

- **Crest** → the raven-and-crystal (club badge)
- **Sponsor** → the `FUTUREPROOF` wordmark (centre chest)
- **Number** → **No. 26** (the year), or personalized

Five kits, mapped to your five hackathon concept slots:

1. **Home — "Salmon Run"** (salmon/ink/aurora) — the signature
2. **Away — "Aurora Curtain"** (parchment/teal) — the wearable one
3. **Third — "Raven & Crystal"** (blackout iridescent + one lime stitch) — the flex
4. **Keeper — "Signal"** (full BC+AI lime) — the loud one
5. **Special — "Space Centre"** (starfield dome, numbered /226) — the collector
