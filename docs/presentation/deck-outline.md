## 1: Cold open
Full-bleed: **WHOSE CUP IS IT ANYWAY?** on ink. No logo yet. Let it sit.

## 2: The setup (the spectacle)
FIFA World Cup 2026 is coming to Vancouver. The flags, the money, the future.
Show the official gloss. (1–2 confirmed facts: matches at BC Place, the dates.)

## 3: The turn (the cost)
Who pays vs. who profits. The public cost figure (confirmed + cited). The pattern:
mega-event arrives, profit privatized, cost socialized, people swept.

## 4: The precedent (Vancouver remembers)
2010 Olympics: the promises vs. the displacement. We have run this play before.
This is the emotional core. Make it specific and sourced.

## 5: The idea
**VANCOUVER MADE**: a protest brand that wears the tournament's language against
itself. Reveal the wordmark + the thesis line.

## 6: The brand
Logo, crest, palette, voice. The "counterfeit official" system. Fast, confident.

## 7: The kits
The hero slides. Home / Away / Future-Forward. Sponsor slot = PUBLIC MONEY.
This is what people screenshot.

## 8: The paraphernalia
Fan Zone / No Zone. Scarf, stickers, posters, the counterfeit trophy.

## 9: The portal (LIVE DEMO)
Switch to the running site. Spin the World Portal. "The game is rigged. Drag-free,
auto-orbit." Land the tagline: *Made in Vancouver, not Vancouver Sold.*

## 10: The ask / close
What this is: protest art, truth to power. Land acknowledgement done with respect.
QR to the live site. Final card: **THE WORLD IS NOT FOR SALE.**
