# Dev Track · We Made the Receipt

**Vancouver Made · BCIT Tech Collider**

They asked for a Vancouver World Cup kit. We finished the sentence. Made on stolen ground,
made on Hogan's Alley, made on $729 million of public money. Everyone else made a souvenir.
We made the receipt.

This isn't a protest about AI. AI's just the brush. The subject is greed, displacement, and
who pays the public bill.

## What it is
One real Vancouver number, said three ways, so it's harder to look away. Take a public-record
receipt about who pays, and put the same truth in three rooms:
- **On the kit:** stitched into the hem, where the sponsor logo should be. (MADE ON, the Pump & Dump FC kit, this site)
- **On the wall:** the bill, pasted up as a poster nobody approved. (feefa.ai)
- **On the body:** shot like couture, captioned like evidence. (world-cup-fashion-cake.vercel.app)

## What to click
`/engine`: pick a Vancouver receipt and watch it land all three ways at once, source attached.

## How it's made
We rebuilt the look of an official World Cup drop, then loaded it with the part they leave
off: the public cost, the inquiry, the neighbourhood that got paved. The numbers come from
public record (`receipts.js`), every one cited and flagged to confirm against a primary
source before it goes out. No real FIFA, club, or brand marks. We build our own and put the
citation on the hem so you can check our math.

AI helped us make a lot of this, fast. It's the brush, not the point. The point is who pays.
