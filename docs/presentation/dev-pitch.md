# Dev Track Pitch · The Receipts Engine

**Devin Open Hackathon · Vancouver Made · BCIT Tech Collider**

## The one-liner
Not a project. A method. We turn Vancouver civic receipts into counter-spectacle
artifacts across three surfaces, with AI as the engine and cited public records plus
human taste as the guardrail. Same fact, three voices, one pipeline.

## What to click
- **The Receipts Engine** at `/engine` (live demo). Pick a Vancouver World Cup receipt;
  watch it render at once as a kit hem citation, a protest poster, and a couture
  editorial caption. Each carries the same source.
- **MADE ON** (this site) — the wearable receipt: the Pump & Dump FC kit + the 9-kit system.
- **feefa.ai** — the civic receipt: host-city accountability as a protest site.
- **world-cup-fashion-cake.vercel.app** — the editorial receipt: stadium infrastructure as couture.

## The move (why it is novel)
Take the World Cup's official spectacle, turn its own language back on it, and let AI do
the production while cited receipts and human judgment hold the line. The novelty is not
any one site. It is that one repeatable method outputs a jersey, a civic site, and a
fashion editorial from the same evidence spine. A new way of making, not a one-off.

## The pipeline
```
Vancouver public record (receipts.js)
   -> AI generation (image / text / 3D)
   -> human taste + citation guardrail
   -> multi-surface artifact (kit · poster · editorial · 3D portal)
```
The Receipts Engine is that pipeline made visible: `src/data/voices.js` encodes the three
house voices; `receipts.js` is the public-record spine; every output is cited.

## How it hits the rubric
- **Novelty of process and output.** One engine, one fact, three surfaces. The system is the pitch.
- **Best use of AI / tools.** Built today, AI-assisted end to end; AI is the production engine, receipts are the brakes.
- **Best Vancouver narrative.** Driven by the $729M public bill, the Oppal inquiry, Hogan's Alley, the 2010 displacement, the VSE.
- **Show your process.** The engine's process panel, the three live properties, and a documented mimic-invert-cite method.
