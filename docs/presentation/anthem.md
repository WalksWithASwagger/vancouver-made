# MADE ON — the anthem

Two Suno-ready versions of the collection anthem, built from the real receipts
(the numbers *are* the hooks — "mimic the polish, invert the payload, bake in the
receipt"). Drop the chosen render at `public/highlight-reel/audio/reel.mp3` and it
auto-scores the [`/highlight-reel`](../../src/components/HighlightReel.jsx) page and
the exported `.mp4`.

Every figure is sourced: $729M gross / $242M security ([receipts.js](../../src/data/receipts.js)),
~4,400 unsold condos + ~12× price-to-income + VSE "scam capital" 1989
([heroKits.js](../../src/data/heroKits.js)), Nardwuar/APEC '97, No.5 Orange, China
Creek bowls 1979, Hogan's Alley razed for the viaduct c.1971 → land trust 2022.

---

## Version A — terrace punk (the spit)

**Suno style:**
```
anthemic protest post-punk, football terrace chant, 142 BPM, motorik drums,
distorted bass, brass stabs, air-horn, shouted gang vocals, half-sung snarl,
lo-fi riso grit, IDLES x Sleaford Mods x FC St. Pauli terrace, defiant,
Vancouver grey turning neon
```

**"NO GAME WITHOUT THE GROUND"** — `(CAPS in parens)` = crowd answer-vocals.

```
[Intro — lone voice, crowd answers, building]
Made on what?  (STOLEN GROUND.)
Made on what?  (HOGAN'S ALLEY.)
Made on what?  (SEVEN-TWENTY-NINE MILLION — OF YOUR MONEY.)
Whistle. Kick-off.

[Verse 1 — spat, fast]
Seven games and the bill runs seven-twenty-nine,
two-forty-two on the fence so the floodlights look fine.
California went private — you swallowed the tab,
grass on top of the transfer, "BANK OF FIFA" on the back.
They want a souvenir — a clean little lie —
we stitch the receipt in the hem, dye-sub it, let it dry.

[Pre-Chorus]
Not anti-football —  (ANTI-AMNESIA!)
You made a souvenir —  (WE MADE THE RECEIPT!)

[Chorus — full gang chant, brass + horn]
NO GAME — WITHOUT — THE GROUND!  (oi! oi!)
NO GAME WITHOUT THE GROUND!
Wear the whole city like a wound —
hold the line, hold the ground —
NO GAME WITHOUT THE GROUND!

[Verse 2 — the clubs, traded fast]
Ninety-seven — Nardwuar — the question you dodge:
who benefits? who pays? keep the whole file lodged.
Number Five Orange — the town's one velvet rope,
"work is work," public land, private champagne smoke.
Seventy-nine in the bowl, China Creek concrete —
the bylaw said NO; the kids poured the receipt.

[Pre-Chorus]
Mimic the polish —  (INVERT THE PAYLOAD!)
Bake it right in —  (BAKE IN THE RECEIPT!)

[Chorus]
NO GAME WITHOUT THE GROUND! ... (repeat, bigger)

[Bridge — drops low, menace — Pump & Dump]
Forty-four hundred condos dark — nobody home,
twelve times the wage just to stand on your own.
"Scam capital" city since '89, same script —
inflate it, extract it, abandon the ship.
You're the bagholder, love — the exit's for the few...
(brass swells, defiant)
...but HOGAN'S ALLEY'S STILL HERE — and the block comes through.

[Outro — chant to fade]
Made on what?  (THE RECEIPT!)
Made on what?  (THE RECEIPT!)
On unceded ground we never bought —
NO GAME... WITHOUT... THE GROUND.
(no game... no game...)
```

## Version B — west-coast protest folk rock (the singalong)

**Suno style:**
```
west coast protest folk rock, Pacific Northwest, 100 BPM, acoustic guitar,
pedal steel, fiddle, harmonica, upright bass, hammond organ, brushed-to-full
drums, gang-harmony singalong, boot stomp + hand claps, Neil Young & Crazy
Horse x The Be Good Tanyas x Spirit of the West, warm, defiant, rain into sun,
builds big
```

**"STILL HERE, STILL BUILDING"**

```
[Intro — fingerpicked guitar + harmonica, soft]
They asked us for the Vancouver story,
so we finished the line they wrote —
made on stolen ground, on Hogan's Alley,
on seven-twenty-nine of public coin.

[Verse 1 — guitar + upright bass, brushed kit]
Seven games and the bill comes due,
seven-twenty-nine, and the lights look new —
two-forty-two for the fence and the floods,
California went private — we got the bill, not the goods.
They sewed a little grass on the top of the take
and called it a souvenir — now that's a hell of a fake.

[Pre-Chorus — fiddle enters, lifting]
We don't burn it down and we don't look away,
we print the receipt and we wear it all day —

[Chorus — full band, gang harmonies]
So sing for the ground that they built it on,
the stolen, the paved-over, the gone —
everyone else made a souvenir,
we made the receipt, and we're standing right here.
Whoa-oh-oh, no game without the ground,
whoa-oh-oh, no game without the ground.

[Verse 2 — storytelling, the clubs]
There's Nardwuar with a question and a stack of old files —
who pays, who profits — he'll sit there a while.
There's a No.5 door where the whole town's a rope,
"work is work," they police the worker and they sell you the show.
And down in the bowl where the concrete don't lie,
the bylaw said no — and the kids built it anyway, riding high.

[Pre-Chorus]
We mimic the polish, we invert the pay,
bake it in the hem so it won't wash away —

[Chorus]
So sing for the ground that they built it on... (repeat, fuller)

[Bridge — drops to guitar + organ, then swells]
Four thousand four hundred windows gone dark,
twelve times a wage just to live near the park —
"scam-capital" city since 'eighty-nine,
they pump it and dump it and you hold the line.
(organ swells, drums build)
But Hogan's Alley — razed for the road in 'seventy-one —
got a land trust now, and a rising sun:
STILL HERE. STILL BUILDING. The block comes home...

[Final Chorus — biggest, hands up]
So sing for the ground that they built it on —
unceded Musqueam, Squamish, Tsleil-Waututh: never bought, never gone.
Everyone else made a souvenir,
we made the receipt, and we're not getting cleared.
Whoa-oh-oh, no game without the ground,
whoa-oh-oh, NO GAME WITHOUT THE GROUND.

[Outro — voices + harmonica, softening]
Made on what?  (the receipt.)
Made on what?  (the receipt.)
Still here... still building...
no game without the ground.
```
