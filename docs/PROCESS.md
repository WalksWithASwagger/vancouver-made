# The process: /process

Source: `src/data/process.js`. Component: `src/components/Process.jsx`.

## The thesis

"The human bookends the machine."

Voice is the input. Judgment is the output gate. The machine is the accelerant between them: provenance in, curation out. That is the anti-slop argument made literal. The page walks one kit, Pump & Dump FC, through all seven stages to show the loop is not autonomous.

## The 7 stages

**01 voice** (human). A stance a machine cannot have: a settler artist refusing to make the celebration jersey. The point of view that everything else derives from. Source: `docs/design/brand-system.md`, `submission-brief.md`.

**02 idea** (human). The voice fans into a concept. Pump & Dump FC: the World Cup as a pump-and-dump scheme. Hype the city, bill the public, let insiders exit. The public is the bagholder. Source: `docs/design/clubs/pump-and-dump-fc.md`.

**03 prompts** (handoff). The concept becomes instructions. Three prompt sets per kit: moodboard, graphic elements, jersey flats. The stance, encoded. Source: `docs/design/prompts/09-pump-and-dump/`.

**04 moodboards** (machine). First generations fix the world the kit lives in: midnight navy, toxic teal and magenta, banknote polish over financial severity. Not the kit yet. Direction.

**05 graphic elements** (machine). Standalone marks generated clean on white: the crest (shield split half soccer ball, half candlestick/condo stack), the faux-sponsor bar, the nameplate that prints a role instead of a name. Source: `docs/design/prompts/09-pump-and-dump/graphic-elements.md`.

**06 jersey flats** (machine). The parts resolve into a kit. Hero third: gold-lit condo skyline at the hem dissolving into market-crash static, teal and magenta cuffs. Front, back, full kit. Source: `docs/design/prompts/09-pump-and-dump/jersey-flats.md`.

**07 the decision** (gate, human). 246 generations rated. The misses killed. The winners locked into the tech pack. What survived: the crest, PUMP & DUMP CAPITAL, the BAGHOLDER nameplate, "BUY THE RUMOUR. SELL THE CITY." Source: `docs/design/kits/MO-09-pump-and-dump.md`.

## The anti-slop argument

The loop is not: prompt, accept, ship. It is: point of view, encode, generate at volume, judge hard, lock the survivors. If the human leaves at stage 02, you get slop. The thesis is that the human closing the gate at stage 07 is what makes the work accountable.
