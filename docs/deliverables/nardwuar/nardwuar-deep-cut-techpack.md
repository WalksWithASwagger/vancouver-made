# Tech Pack — NARDWUAR FC · "Deep Cut Home Kit" (red)

**Collection:** VANCOUVER MADE / MADE ON · **Style No:** NW-01 · **Colorway:** Home (Vancouver Tartan red)
**Designathon hero — Fashion Design Track.** Master spec: [`docs/research/tech-pack-spec.md`](../../research/tech-pack-spec.md). Club spec: [`src/data/clubs.js`](../../../src/data/clubs.js) → `nardwuar-fc`.

> **Concept:** Reads as a host-nation home kit — bold primaries, classic striping, crest, World Cup patch — in a tartan-inspired palette ghosted into the fabric like a clean check. Up close it's a walking archive: an ultra-faint all-over collage of 7" records, photocopied flyers and interview microtext. Homage to Nardwuar the Human Serviette. *Research is the protest; the receipt is the weapon.*

## 2. Silhouette & construction

- **Silhouette:** unisex relaxed match-fit, **short-sleeve, set-in** — the all-over collage + tartan check want an unbroken canvas (per master spec §1 per-kit logic; the ¾ raglan signature is dropped here so the print runs uninterrupted).
- **Sizes:** XS–3XL (grade per §2.6), women's + youth cut as options.
- **Neckline:** crew, self-fabric bound (lets the check wrap edge-to-edge). Inside collar reads `DOOT DOOLA DOOT DOO`.
- **Construction:** overlock seams, coverstitch hems, flatlock at skin-contact zones, tonal thread (§8).

## 3. Materials / BOM

- **Body:** ocean-bound / Parley-style recycled PET interlock, **~150 gsm**, optical-bright, sublimation-ready (§3.1). *Material as message: made on ocean plastic.*
- **Shorts:** ocean-bound recycled PET interlock, ~180 gsm (§3.3).
- **Trim:** tartan-green self-fabric or 1×1 rib; elastic waistband + internal drawcord.
- **Recycled content / certs:** spec to GRS/Parley per §3.5; confirm gsm on lab-dip (§3.2).

## 5. Graphics placement

- **Front:** `VANCOUVER` host-nation wordmark across upper chest (no "'26"); `WHO BENEFITS? WHO PAYS?` sponsor bar center-chest; mic-and-records collage crest left chest (host-city shield; **no human face — objects only**); `DEEP RESEARCH` Fair-Play patch right sleeve.
- **Back:** `NARDWUAR!!` nameplate (cut-and-paste ransom-note); `97` below, built from tiny record-sleeve / zine-panel rectangles; back-neck badge `NO GAME WITHOUT THE GROUND`.
- **Shorts:** tartan-red, green/yellow side stripe, small collage crest left leg. **Socks:** tartan bands + a column of `DOOT DOOLA DOOT DOO` microtext down the shin.

## 6. Name & number standard (FIFA-safe defaults, §6)

- **Back number:** 25–35 cm tall, stroke 3–5 cm. **Back name:** 5–7.5 cm, ≥4 cm above number.
- No front number (the `VANCOUVER` wordmark holds the chest). Letters same colour as number, Latin chars, fully visible when tucked.
- **Application:** sublimated (zero hand, pre-planned) to keep the ransom-note collage intact.

## 7. Hem citation (the receipt)

> APEC 1997, VANCOUVER · NARDWUAR ASKED PM CHRÉTIEN ABOUT PEPPER SPRAY ON PROTESTERS · "FOR ME, PEPPER, I PUT IT ON MY PLATE" · AHEAD OF THE HUGHES/APEC INQUIRY INTO RCMP CONDUCT
> Source: Nardwuar footage · The Canadian Encyclopedia · CBC archives (AL-NW-001)

Every on-garment claim carries its citation; nothing unverified ships (AL-NW-001/002 in `clubs.js`).

## 8. Labels & legal (§7)

- Tagless heat-transfer care/size at neck + small woven brand flag.
- **Territorial acknowledgement on the neck label:** made on the unceded territories of the xʷməθkʷəy̓əm (Musqueam), Sḵwx̱wú7mesh (Squamish), and səlilwətaɬ (Tsleil-Waututh) peoples.
- **No real FIFA / club / sponsor marks** — evoke the grammar, never reproduce a real mark. FIFA "Fair Play" badge is re-spoofed as `DEEP RESEARCH`.

## 9. Ethics (hard constraint)

Nardwuar is a living, beloved Vancouverite. **HOMAGE, not likeness** — no face anywhere on the garment or in renders; do not commercialize his name. Channel the ethos (research → truth to power). If this moves past exhibition toward merch, get Nardwuar's blessing first.
