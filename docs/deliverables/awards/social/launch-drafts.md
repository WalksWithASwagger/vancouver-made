## Newsletter / blog post (long-form)

**Title:** Everyone else made a souvenir. We made the receipt.
**Subtitle / dek:** MADE ON is live — a World Cup protest collection that just took double silver at Vancouver Made.

---

The brief at Vancouver Made — the World Cup Designathon and Hackathon at BCIT Tech Collider — was simple: what if Vancouver had its own World Cup kit.

Everyone else made a souvenir. We made the receipt.

MADE ON is a protest collection for the 2026 World Cup. Not a kit that celebrates the tournament — a set of kits that name what it's built on, with every factual claim cited on the hem so you can check our math. Made on stolen ground. Made on Hogan's Alley. Made on $729 million of public money.

It's live now, and you can walk through the whole thing: **vancouver-made.vercel.app**.

**Four clubs, four arguments.** Each design is its own immersive page — the concept, the kit up close, the process, and the receipts behind it:

- **Nardwuar FC** — the red Vancouver-tartan "Deep Cut" home kit. Research as the protest, the receipt as the weapon. This is the one that won, and the one Formmé is manufacturing five of.
- **Pump & Dump FC** — the mega-event as a pump and dump. Hype the city, bill the public, take the exit. You're the bagholder.
- **Number Five Orange** — work is work. The labour the spectacle is built on.
- **China Creek** — public land, defended, then sold. A manifesto in concrete grey and caution yellow.

There's also a Receipts Engine that takes one civic number and renders it three ways, a generative wall of every frame we made, and a making-of for each kit — prompt, mood, marks, flats, and finally the kit on the body.

**It took double silver.** Second in both tracks at Vancouver Made: the Devin Open Hackathon (for the build) and the Formmé Fashion Design challenge (for the kits). Second out of a strong field in each. The design prize is real — Formmé is producing five of the Nardwuar kit.

AI was the brush, not the artist. The subject is greed, displacement, and who pays the public bill. They asked for the Vancouver story. We finished the sentence.

Go see it: **vancouver-made.vercel.app**. Whose cup is it anyway?

*Thanks to Devin by Cognition and Formmé for the tracks, BCIT Tech Collider for the room, and Young Guns and the Students for AI crew for putting it on.*
