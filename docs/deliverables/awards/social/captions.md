## LinkedIn

We won. Twice.

At Vancouver Made, the World Cup Designathon and Hackathon at BCIT Tech Collider this weekend, MADE ON took second in both tracks: the Devin Open Hackathon and the Formmé Fashion Design challenge.

MADE ON isn't a souvenir. The brief was "what if Vancouver had its own World Cup kit." We made the receipt instead: a protest collection that names what the tournament is built on. Made on stolen ground. Made on Hogan's Alley. Made on $729 million of public money. Every claim cited on the hem.

The winning kit is Nardwuar FC, red Vancouver tartan, research as the protest. Formmé is manufacturing five of them.

Built in a day, AI as the brush, not the artist. Thanks to Devin by Cognition and Formmé for the tracks, BCIT Tech Collider for the room, and Young Guns and the Students for AI crew for putting it on.

Whose cup is it anyway?
