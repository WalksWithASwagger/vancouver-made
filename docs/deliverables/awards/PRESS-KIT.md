A one-page kit for media, sponsors, and the Formmé production relationship. Everything here is
factual and shareable. Voice: plain, no hype.

## The headline
MADE ON won double silver at Vancouver Made, the World Cup Designathon and Hackathon at BCIT Tech
Collider, June 20 2026: second place in both tracks.

| Track | Result | Field | Prize |
|-------|--------|-------|-------|
| Devin Open Hackathon | 2nd place | ~100 entrants | $300 |
| Formmé Fashion Design | 2nd place | ~50 entrants | Five kits manufactured |

## What MADE ON is
A protest collection for the 2026 World Cup. The brief was "what if Vancouver had its own kit."
MADE ON answered with the receipt instead of the souvenir: a set of football kits that name what
the tournament is built on, with every claim cited on the hem. Made on stolen ground. Made on
Hogan's Alley. Made on $729 million of public money. AI was the brush, not the artist.

## The winning kit
NARDWUAR FC, the red Vancouver-tartan "Deep Cut" home kit. Research as the protest, the receipt as
the weapon. Formmé is manufacturing five.
