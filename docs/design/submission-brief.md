## The Four Questions (LOCKED)

### 1. What part of Vancouver are you representing?

**The spectacle itself.** The gap between the broadcast version and what it actually costs. Specifically: the World Cup 2026 as a pump-and-dump cycle: hype the asset (the city, the tournament), bill the public, take the exit liquidity, leave the bagholders. This is the mechanism that runs underneath gentrification, mega-events, and real-estate financialization in Vancouver.

The specific wound: ~$320–338M public cost + $67–74M other public-sector spending + ~$242M security, while ~2,500 condos sit unsold and the price-to-income ratio is ~12× (Demographia 2025) — among the world's most unaffordable. Foreign capital flipped the market; the World Cup is the press release that moved the price. The residents are the retail buyers left holding the bag after the smart money exited.

### 2. What story or feeling does the design communicate?

**Without the presentation, just the jersey itself:**

You're wearing a blackout kit dressed as a hyped 2026 "limited-edition city pride drop": the kind every host city releases to monetize nationalism. But the longer you look, the jersey resolves into a prospectus. The all-over pattern is soaring then cliff-diving line charts. The sponsor bar says `PUMP & DUMP CAPITAL / WE WIN IF YOU LIVE HERE OR NOT`. The crest is a condo tower stacked like a candlestick chart. The shorthand on the socks is price-to-income ratios: `22×`, `33×`.

**The feeling:** vertigo. Pride and complicity, side by side. The jersey looks like you're celebrating something. Until you read it, and you realize you're wearing the mechanism that displaced you.

### 3. How does the design connect back to the city?

**Could this jersey only come from Vancouver?**

Yes. This argument only lands in Vancouver in 2026 because:
- **The VSE history is real.** "Scam Capital of the World" (Forbes 1989) is not metaphor. It's how the stock exchange was actually branded. Howe Street jargon ("pump and dump," "forward-looking statements," "exit liquidity") is Vancouver native.
- **The housing specificity is unavoidable.** A ~12× price-to-income multiple (Demographia 2025) is Vancouver-specific; the World Cup cost figures are specific; the BCSC 2026 pump-and-dump finding is real. A jersey with a generic chart could come from any city. A jersey with these numbers and this history could only come from here.
- **The spectacle is live.** The tournament is actually happening in 2026; the hype is actually moving the market. The jersey isn't a historical critique. It's a real-time indictment.
- **Adbusters lineage is local.** The brand-hijack technique (mimicry + inverted payload) is Vancouver homegrown (Kalle Lasn, 1989). This jersey is wearing the city's own protest tradition.

### 4. Why would someone be proud to wear it?

Pride = it says something true about the wearer. Someone wearing Pump & Dump FC is saying:

- *"I see the mechanism. I'm not pretending the city is just a beautiful place. It's also a market being played."*
- *"I live here despite the squeeze, and I'm not celebrating the tournament while the city prices out everyone I know."*
- *"The smart money took the exit liquidity. I'm the bagholder. This jersey names that."*
- *"Vancouver has always been this honest about being built on extraction: settlers, gold, timber, real estate. At least we're not pretending anymore."*

It's proud because it **refuses the souvenir**. Everyone else makes the celebration jersey. This one makes the receipt. Wearing it means you're on the side of the people who read the fine print.

## Visual Direction

- **Palette:** Midnight navy body with toxic-neon teal & magenta gradients (the hyped 2026 promo energy)
- **All-over print:** Soaring then cliff-diving line charts; condo towers (lit vs. dark); annotations (`2021 PEAK`, `FLIP TAX`, `FOREIGN BUYER SURCHARGE`)
- **Crest:** Shield split: soccer ball / candlestick chart + condo stack
- **Sponsor bar:** `PUMP & DUMP CAPITAL` / `WE WIN IF YOU LIVE HERE OR NOT`
- **Name/number:** Roles, not players: `DEVELOPER`, `FLIPPER`, `LANDLORD`, `RENOVICTOR`; numbers from floor-plans / MLS codes
- **Shorts/socks:** Shorts = `VACANT` stamp pattern; socks = price-to-income ratios (`22×`, `33×`)
- **Hem citation:** `SECURITY $242M · NET TO BC TAXPAYER UP TO $114M · CALIFORNIA WENT PRIVATE · YOU DIDN'T` (source: CBC / Globe and Mail, 2026)
