# TECH PACK: NW-01 · NARDWUAR FC — "DEEP CUT" (Vancouver Tartan)

Collection: MADE ON · Series: *Companion clubs (ALLEY LEAGUE)* ·
Designer: Kris Krüg · Category: soccer kit — jersey + match shorts · Fit: unisex relaxed match-fit · XS–3XL.
Data: `src/data/clubs.js#nardwuar-fc` · Concept brief: `docs/design/clubs/nardwuar-fc.md`.

**Silhouette:** **short-sleeve set-in** (clean sleeve panel so the DEEP RESEARCH patch and
the all-over collage read uninterrupted). V-neck, self-fabric bound. See [tech-pack §1 + §2](../../research/tech-pack-spec.md).
**Material:** **ocean-bound recycled-PET interlock** (the base fabric carries the *made on what's discarded* message). See [§3](../../research/tech-pack-spec.md#3-materials--bom).

**Concept (one line):** A World Cup home kit turned into a walking archive of the city's
underground — *research is the protest; the receipt is the weapon.* **The line:** *Do the
homework, then ask power the thing it's dodging.*

An affectionate homage to **Nardwuar the Human Serviette** (hyper-researched guerrilla
interviewer, frontman of The Evaporators, rooted at CiTR/UBC). **Homage, not likeness** — the
tribute is carried entirely through objects (the tam, the mic, the records, the press pass),
never his face or name-as-merch. See **Ethics gate** below.

## Print method
Ghosted tartan check + all-over collage = **dye-sublimation**, edge-to-edge, before cut-and-sew.
Hem micro-type = fine screen / high-density heat transfer, **≥6–8 pt vector**, proofed on the
real blank. **Limited edition:** metallic-gold **foil / heat-transfer** on crest, wordmark,
DEEP RESEARCH patch and number (low-flex zones only). Sleeve patch = woven badge, merrow edge.

## Why it wins
Answers the brief — *could this kit only come from Vancouver?* — yes: it is Vancouver's
underground canon (CiTR, the punk venues, the question power was dodging) worn on the body.
Reads as a bold tartan host-nation home kit from across the room; up close it's a riso-zine
walking archive. The methodology of the whole project — *do the homework, ask the hard
question* — made wearable.

## Ethics gate
Affectionate tribute to a **living, beloved Vancouverite** — he is the hero, power is the
target. Channel the ethos (research → truth to power); **do not impersonate his likeness or
commercialize his name.** No face in any artwork. **If this moves past exhibition toward merch,
get Nardwuar's blessing first** (he's active and approachable). This gate governs the queued
Shopify build.
