# TECH PACK: MO-10 · MADE ON HOGAN'S ALLEY

Collection: MADE ON · Series: *what the city is built on* · Designer: Kris Krüg
(settler artist, in solidarity) · Category: soccer jersey (home) · Fit: unisex relaxed
match-fit · XS–3XL. Data: `src/data/heroKits.js#hogans-alley` (+ deep dive
`src/data/clubs.js#hogans-alley-fc`).

**Silhouette:** **short / set-in sleeve, classic cut** — homage to the great African
national kits. **Material:** **ocean-bound recycled-PET interlock** base + **metallic-gold
sublimation** + **original geometric jacquard trim** (*made on what's discarded; built to
return*).

**Concept (one line):** The neighbourhood the city paved over, worn as a future — memorial
and blueprint. **The line:** *The block comes home.*

> Memorial + generative — the collection's only kit in the future tense. Honours an erased
> Black neighbourhood and hands the spotlight to the people rebuilding it.

## Print method
Metallic-gold + star-map gradients + allover weave/grid = dye-sublimation (gold via
metallic sublimation channel if Formme runs it; else spot metallic). Hem micro-type =
fine screen / heat transfer.

## Why it wins
The only kit that ends in the future tense. It names an erasure the city now admits —
"Made on Hogan's Alley" is already in the brand triad — then routes the spotlight to the
people rebuilding the block. Gold where the others are blackout; memorial and blueprint at
once. The "sponsor" slot honours a community institution instead of mocking a corporation —
the inversion of the collection's own inversion.

## Manifesto card
Side A: the MADE ON statement. Side B: the Hogan's Alley timeline (1900s → c.1971 → 2022 →
ahead) + a QR to **Hogan's Alley Society** and **Ethọ́s Lab** — attention and any proceeds
routed to them.
