# Kit & Paraphernalia — VANCOUVER MADE FC

The kits are the centerpiece deliverable. Each is a wearable protest poster that
passes as a football shirt. Three kits + a paraphernalia line.

## 01 — Home Kit · "THE DISPLACED XI"
- **Base:** rain blue-grey (`#1c2b33`).
- **Primary:** hi-vis hazard orange (`#ff3b00`) — the color of safety vests,
  caution tape, the people doing the actual labor.
- **Sponsor slot:** reads **PUBLIC MONEY** in official sponsor type.
- **Numbers:** styled as eviction-notice / case numbers.
- **Collar tag:** *Civitas Pro Populo.*
- **Detail:** sleeve patch = a tent silhouette where the federation badge goes.

## 02 — Away Kit · "THE SWEEP"
- **Base:** newsprint bone (`#f4f1ea`).
- **Trim:** counterfeit gold (`#d9a521`) + tarp blue accent.
- **Back of shirt:** a redacted address (an SRO / encampment site), bars over it.
- **Front:** faint halftone of a crowd being moved along.
- **Concept:** the "clean" broadcast jersey — literally whitewashed.

## 03 — Third Kit · "FUTURE FORWARD" (the portal kit)
- **Base:** ink black with cyan (`#21d9c9`) circuitry lines = the World Portal.
- **Concept:** the techno-optimist future they sell us; glitchy, beautiful, hollow.
- Ties the physical kit to the 3D portal on the site.

## Paraphernalia line — "FAN ZONE / NO ZONE"
Every item doubles as a flyer / piece of evidence.
- [ ] **Scarf** — "WHOSE CUP IS IT ANYWAY?" terrace-scarf format.
- [ ] **Sticker sheet** — slogans from `tokens.js`, slap-tag format.
- [ ] **Riso posters** (3) — match-poster parody w/ real cost figures.
- [ ] **Counterfeit trophy** — desktop object / prop, "WORLD CUP OF DISPLACEMENT."
- [ ] **Matchday programme** — zine of the research, sold as a "fan guide."
- [ ] **Ticket** — admits one to "a city you can no longer afford."
