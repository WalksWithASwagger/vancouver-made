# China Creek — Mood Board (Rafiki / Nano Banana Pro)

Reference plates: clean city-pride home-kit polish over East Van skate-concrete grit. Generate
with `--style china-creek -m pro -r 1K`. Palette (concrete grey / marker black / caution yellow
/ creek teal) and ethics (punch up, no faces) live in the style suffix; these prompts set
subject + composition only. No human faces anywhere.

## 1. Concrete bowl, coping and transition
**For:** All-over body texture, the concrete colourway
**Aspect Ratio:** 4:5
**Prompt:**
> Extreme close-up of a weathered concrete skatepark bowl, the coping lip and a smooth transition curve, wax marks, scuffs and hairline cracks, faint caution-yellow paint flecks, raw East Vancouver DIY concrete, hard daylight. Full-bleed.

## 2. "NO SKATEBOARDING" bylaw sign
**For:** Front sponsor mark reference, the bylaw-as-brand device
**Aspect Ratio:** 4:5
**Prompt:**
> Macro of a municipal "NO SKATEBOARDING" prohibition sign, the pictogram bold and official, scratched and sticker-covered metal, caution yellow and marker black, bureaucratic city-signage texture. The sign reads as a brand mark.

## 3. Griptape and deck underside
**For:** Texture layer, cuff and trim reference
**Aspect Ratio:** 4:5
**Prompt:**
> Flat-lay close-up of a skateboard deck underside and black griptape, peeling stickers, marker scrawl, worn edges and chipped ply, DIY screen-print texture, tactile skate-culture object study.

## 4. Skate-zine cut-and-paste
**For:** All-over ghost layer, hem and number fill
**Aspect Ratio:** 4:5
**Prompt:**
> A layered skate-zine cut-and-paste collage, photocopied flyers and ransom-note headlines, heavy xerox grain, toner-black ink on bone newsprint with caution-yellow accents, DIY punk screenprint grit, slight misregistration. No faces.

## 5. Spray-stencil on raw concrete
**For:** Accent treatment, slogan/arrow language
**Aspect Ratio:** 4:5
**Prompt:**
> Spray-stencil graffiti on a raw concrete wall, hard-edged stencil arrows and bars, caution yellow and creek teal over grey, overspray haze and drips, East Van underpass texture, weathered and tactile.

## 6. Bowl-contour transition map
**For:** All-over print pattern, number treatment
**Aspect Ratio:** 4:5
**Prompt:**
> A clean line-art transition map of nested skatepark bowl contours and coping curves, thin caution-yellow and creek-teal lines on concrete grey, technical topographic feel, tight enough to read as an all-over fabric pattern.

## 7. Bowl-and-skateboard crest still life
**For:** Crest inner art, trophy-misuse (skateboard deck) mark
**Aspect Ratio:** 4:5
**Prompt:**
> A graphic still life: a skatepark bowl cross-section with a coping line beside a skateboard deck silhouette and a spray can. Objects only, no person, no face. Marker-black and caution-yellow on concrete grey, clean prop lighting, emblematic icon study.

## 8. Skate-tourism brochure, inverted
**For:** "Skate Tourism" third-kit reference, the polish to subvert
**Aspect Ratio:** 4:5
**Prompt:**
> Flat lay of a glossy city skate-tourism brochure, aspirational destination-marketing layout, clean sans-serif headlines and gold accents on cream, sanctioned and too-polished, the official gloss the kit will turn against itself. No faces.
