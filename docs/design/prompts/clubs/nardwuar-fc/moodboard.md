# Mood Board Prompts: NARDWUAR FC

**Goal:** Establish the visual language for "the walking archive." Reads as a host-nation
home kit — bold tartan primaries, classic broadcast polish — then resolves into a
cut-and-paste fanzine of Vancouver's underground. Official broadcast accreditation,
flipped into unsanctioned press.

**Aesthetic:** Tartan home-kit gravity + riso-printed zine grit + cassette/7"-single
archaeology. Civic PR reel on the surface; the deep-cut counter-reel underneath.

**Ethos, not likeness:** This is an **homage to a living person** — never his face.
Carry it through the tam silhouette, the records, the zines, the mic stand. No portrait,
no caricature, no recognizable human likeness in any prompt.

**MJ settings:** `--ar 4:5 --style raw` (portrait, documentary vibe, no artistic filter)

**Palette:** tartan red `#c8102e` · vinyl ink `#0a0a0a` · tartan green `#1d7a46` · tartan yellow `#e8c531`

## Synthesis Notes

Once the mood board lands, look for:
- **Dominant colour trio:** tartan red + green + yellow confirmed, on vinyl black/bone.
- **Texture language:** broadcast polish on top, riso-zine grit underneath — the
  two-distance trick (clean tartan home kit at 10m, fanzine archive at arm's length).
- **Typography feel:** classic athletic broadcast caps blended with cut-and-paste ransom note.
- **The rule:** evoke the official tournament home kit; never reproduce real marks. And
  never a face — the ethos rides on objects, not the man.
