# Jersey Flats Prompts — NUMBER FIVE ORANGE

**Goal:** Generate complete jersey flat mockups (front / back / side, technical
illustration style) for the full **away / home / third** family, ready for presentation
or iteration.

**Strategy:** Technical fashion-illustration prompts that read as a glam, broadcast-ready
away strip from across the room — and resolve into a labour manifest up close.

**Ethics:** Punch up — **never caricature the workers**. Number silhouettes stay abstract
and faceless. No reproduced landmark photos.

**Palette:** safety-cone orange `#ff6a00` · vinyl black `#0a0a0a` · merch gold `#d9a521` · neon marquee pink `#ff2d6f`

## Iteration Strategy

**Round 1 (Prompts 1–3):** Lock the hero AWAY strip — front, back, sleeve/collar — in the
orange/white/gold colourway. This sets the glam broadcast read.

**Round 2 (Prompts 4–7):** Generate the HOME (vinyl-black surcharge) and THIRD ("Champagne
Room Economy") family members.

**Round 3 (Prompts 8–9):** Detail close-ups — the crest, and the silhouette-number reveal
that flips the glam read into the labour manifest.

**Round 4 (Prompt 10):** Full kit (jersey + shorts + socks) as the closing composition.

## Technical Notes

- **White background:** essential for clean extraction onto presentation materials.
- **No caricature:** review every generation — the number's human silhouettes must stay
  abstract and faceless. This is non-negotiable per the ethics note in
  `../../../../src/data/clubs.js` (sex work = labour, risk, criminalization; punch up at
  buyers and bylaw, not down at the strip).
- **Receipt on the hem:** landmark citation (`AL-N5-001`) or the HRAP policy line (`AL-N5-002`).
  Cross-check the wording against `clubs.js` before generating — no stat drifts from source.
- **Register:** "technical flat-lay fashion illustration", "professional sports apparel",
  "ready for production" — avoid "photo-realistic".
