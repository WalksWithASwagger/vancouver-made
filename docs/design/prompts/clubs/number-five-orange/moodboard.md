# Mood Board Prompts: NUMBER FIVE ORANGE

**Goal:** Establish the visual language for "the whole city becomes a VIP room." Reads as
a glam, broadcast-ready World Cup away strip on TV — the highlight-reel version that fills
the hotel rooms. Up close it resolves into a labour manifest and an itemized cover charge.

**Aesthetic:** Premium "official drop" gloss (foil, hospitality-tier sheen, limited-edition
stamp) bent into the language of a cover charge and a VIP rope. Safety-cone orange façade
over a thermal-receipt underbelly.

**Ethics:** Punch up at the buyers, VIP culture and the bylaw — **never caricature the
workers**. Sex work is labour, risk, criminalization. Any human reference stays an
abstract silhouette. Landmark photos are reference-only: illustrate, never reproduce.

**MJ settings:** `--ar 4:5 --style raw` (portrait, documentary vibe, no artistic filter)

**Palette:** safety-cone orange `#ff6a00` · vinyl black `#0a0a0a` · merch gold `#d9a521` · neon marquee pink `#ff2d6f`

## Synthesis Notes

Once the mood board lands, look for:
- **Dominant colour set:** safety-cone orange + white + merch gold, with neon-pink signal.
- **Texture language:** premium official gloss on the surface, thermal-receipt grit
  underneath — the two-distance trick (glam away strip at 10m, labour manifest at arm's length).
- **Typography feel:** clean carrier/airline sponsor wordmark blended with thermal-receipt mono.
- **The line that must land:** the night was always billed to someone — and it wasn't the buyer.
- **The rule:** evoke the official away kit; never reproduce real marks. Keep every human
  an abstract silhouette — punch up, never down.
