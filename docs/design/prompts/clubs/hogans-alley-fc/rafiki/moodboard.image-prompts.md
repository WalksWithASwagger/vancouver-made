# Hogan's Alley FC — Moodboard

Reference plates for a memorial / Afrofuturist heritage football kit honouring Hogan's Alley,
Vancouver's historic Black neighbourhood in Strathcona, cleared by urban renewal and the Georgia
Viaduct around 1967–71 — now being reclaimed (Hogan's Alley Society, Community Land Trust, Black
Cultural Centre). Palette: deep cosmic indigo, metallic jazz-brass gold, cosmic violet, bone.
Aesthetic in the lineage of great African national kits (Zaire '74, Nigeria '94/'18) — heritage
polish, not pastiche. **Reference the culture respectfully: NO sacred or specific cultural motifs,
NO real people, NO faces, NO text other than what's specified.** This is a tribute, not ownership.
