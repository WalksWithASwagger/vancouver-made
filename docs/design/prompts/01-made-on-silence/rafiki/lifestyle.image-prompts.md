# Made On Silence — Lifestyle / On-Body

Editorial, photoreal on-body shots of the **Made On Silence** kit: a black / charcoal soccer
jersey crossed by heavy white-and-black **redaction bars**, FOI/government-document text, boxed
"REDACTED" nameplates. The concept is about **censorship and the silenced public record** —
punch up at the institutions doing the redacting, never at residents. Models are generic,
anonymous, diverse, not resembling any real person. Photoreal, 35mm editorial fashion
photography, film grain, Vancouver settings. The kit must read black with bold redaction bars.
