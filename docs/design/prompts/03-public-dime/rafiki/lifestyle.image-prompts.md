# Public Dime FC — Lifestyle / On-Body

with gold "BANK OF FIFA" / "LEGAL TENDER FOR ALL DEBTS, PUBLIC" sponsor lockup, gold banknote
engraving, and a "729,000,000" treatment. The concept satirizes **public money funding private
spectacle** — punch up at the institutions billing the public, never at residents. Models are
generic, anonymous, diverse, not resembling any real person. Photoreal, 35mm editorial fashion
