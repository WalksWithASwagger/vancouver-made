## Truth to power
The China Creek bowls (East Vancouver, built 1979) are a monument to public space
won the hard way and held by its users ever since. For decades the city treated
street skating as a nuisance: banned it, policed it, swept it. Then it built the
parks, sold Vancouver as a skate-tourism destination, and cheered skateboarding
into the Olympics. The Cup runs the same play on a bigger field: criminalize a
subculture, then sell it back as flavour. *Who benefits:* the city tourism brand,
developers eyeing the land, event and Olympic sponsors. *Who pays:* local skaters,
the DIY builders, the East Van kids policed off the street and priced out of the
next version of their own ground.

The kit takes the thing a host city loves to brand (its "authentic, gritty street
culture") and makes you read the bylaw underneath it. The home strip is the city's
proud face: the version on the tourism reel. Up close it resolves into a record of
who was allowed to use public space, and on what terms.

## How it steals FIFA 2026
Classic World Cup **home-kit template**: clean paneling, host-city sleeve patch,
trophy watermark. But the host-city crest reads `VANCOUVER 2026 · UNOFFICIAL`, and
the big sponsor block is the city's own **NO SKATEBOARDING** bylaw sign, set like a
kit-maker mark. Official "city pride" energy (the foil, the limited-edition gloss)
gets bent into the language of a no-skating pictogram and a defended bowl.

## The faux-sponsor board
Placed exactly where a real kit's commercial inventory sits. Read top to bottom,
it's the city's own playbook: police it, then sell it.
- **Front sponsor (chest):** `NO SKATEBOARDING`, the bylaw pictogram set like a brand.
- **Sleeve cuff:** `PUBLIC LAND / PRIVATE PROFIT` over `BUILT 1979 / DEFENDED EVER SINCE`.
- **Back-neck micro-sponsor:** `DIY OR DIE`.
- **Shorts tag:** `SKATE TOURISM DESTINATION (NOW)`.
- **Alt / protest slot:** `CRIMINALIZED THEN MONETIZED`.

## Tech-pack beats
- **Base:** raw-concrete grey body, caution-yellow city-signage trim, marker-black detailing.
- **Chest:** a creek-teal coping line sweeps the torso like the lip of a bowl wall.
- **Numbers:** built from bowl-contour and coping curves over a griptape grid, a
  transition map where the digits should be.
- **Patch spoof:** where "FIFA Quality Pro" sits, `NUISANCE BYLAW APPROVED`.
- **Trophy misuse:** trophy silhouette becomes a skateboard deck (or a spray can,
  or a bowl coping curve).
- **Collar:** a stencilled "no skating" band, the pictogram repeated and crossed out.
- **Inside hem:** a printed timeline (banned, swept, parked, branded, Olympic), the
  same ground sold back at every step.
- **Swing tag / hangtag:** styled as a parks-permit / closure notice (*"This space
  reserved. You built it."*), carrying the receipts (below) on the reverse.
- **Care label:** `HOLD THE GROUND THE WAY THEY HELD THE BRAND`.
- **Back-neck badge:** `NO GAME WITHOUT THE GROUND`.

## Kit family (canonical in `clubs.js`)

## Ethics (owner is final guard)
Punch up at the bylaw, redevelopment pressure, and the tourism and Olympic
monetization of a policed subculture. The skaters, the DIY builders and East Van
youth are the **home team, never the joke.** No reproduced FIFA or Olympic marks,
evoke the system in our own voice.
