## Truth to power
Hogan's Alley was Vancouver's most prominent Black neighbourhood — Black-owned
businesses, the AME Fountain Chapel, and the city's jazz heart, in Strathcona from the
early 1900s. It was cleared through urban renewal and the building of the Georgia
Viaduct around 1967–71. **"Made on Hogan's Alley" is already one of the three answers
in the MADE ON triad** (`collection.js#brand.answer`) — but until now no kit carried it.

This one does, and it refuses to end on the wound. A **2022 land trust** is bringing the
block home — affordable housing and a **Black Cultural Centre** — and **Ethọ́s Lab** is
building Afrofuturist Black-youth futures in the city right now. Grief in gold, and the
block coming home.

*Who benefited:* the mid-century freeway / urban-renewal agenda; property and
redevelopment interests; a city that paved over its own Black history; the souvenir
Vancouver that leaves it out. *Who paid:* the Black families displaced; Vancouver's
Black cultural memory and its descendants; a jazz district erased from the broadcast.

## How it steals FIFA 2026
Host-city kits flatten local culture into friendly merch (see Ilanaaq, 2010). This
refuses the flattening. It uses the *exact* polish of an official heritage kit — but in
service of a reclamation, not a souvenir: it puts a neighbourhood the city bulldozed back
on the official map, in future tense.

## Tech-pack beats
- **Base:** deep cosmic indigo with metallic-gold engraving (a star-map gradient).
- **All-over print:** an original geometric weave + an Afrofuturist star-map; a ghosted
  jazz-era street grid of the old neighbourhood. *Original geometry only — no appropriated
  sacred or specific cultural motifs.*
- **Crest:** a brass jazz horn and a rising star over the reclaimed block; ribbon `STILL HERE`.
- **Name/number:** numbers drawn from the Hogan's Alley street grid and the land-trust
  floor-plans — the block redrawn. The nameplate honours a community name **chosen with
  Hogan's Alley Society, not assigned by us**.
- **Shorts/socks:** the land-trust site plan as architectural linework; socks carry the
  timeline (1900s → 1971 → 2022 → ahead).
- **Trophy misuse:** the trophy silhouette becomes a brass horn / the rising tower of the
  new Black Cultural Centre.
- **Hangtag:** a mock "deed" — *the block, returned* — with the receipts and a QR to
  Hogan's Alley Society and Ethọ́s Lab on the reverse.
- **Care label:** `HANDLE THE HISTORY WITH CARE. THIS BLOCK IS NOT FOR SALE.`

## Ethics (owner is final guard)
A **settler artist amplifying, not authoring.** This kit is a tribute to Hogan's Alley and
a signal-boost for the people rebuilding it — not a claim on a story that isn't mine to
tell. Credit and link **Hogan's Alley Society** and **Ethọ́s Lab**; route attention and any
proceeds to them; reference the culture, never appropriate sacred or specific motifs.
**Before this moves past exhibition toward anything public or sold, it should be shown to —
and shaped by — the community, with their blessing first.** (Same discipline as Nardwuar
FC: homage, not ownership.)
