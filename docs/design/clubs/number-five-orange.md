# NUMBER FIVE ORANGE — "Work Is Work Away Strip"

**Hero kit:** AWAY · **No.5** · Target: nightlife capitalism, the spectacle economy,
sex-work stigma. Data: `src/data/clubs.js#number-five-orange`.

> **TV read:** a glam World Cup away strip — crisp whites, gold trim, a punchy
> orange. **Street read:** a jersey for the people whose labour props up the
> city's nightlife while policy and stigma leave them exposed.

## Truth to power
No.5 Orange (Main & Powell) — the old Melbourne Hotel (1904), turned No.5 Orange in
1971 — is one of the last of old Gastown, a landmark that's hosted everyone from
Gretzky to Bon Jovi. The city sells "edgy nightlife" to tourists and stag parties
while criminalizing and policing the people who do the work. The Cup turns public
land into a private champagne room: **a cover charge at the door of your own city.**
*Who benefits:* club/hotel owners, FIFA hospitality, stag tourism. *Who pays:* sex
workers (labour, risk, criminalization), service staff, swept residents.

The kit takes the one thing a host city is most eager to sell — its "edgy,
world-class nightlife" — and makes you read the receipt for it. The away strip is
the broadcast-friendly face: the version on the highlight reel, the version that
fills the hotel rooms. Up close it resolves into a labour manifest.

## How it steals FIFA 2026
Classic World Cup **away-kit template** — clean paneling, host-city sleeve patch,
trophy watermark — but the host-city crest reads **VANCOUVER 2026 — UNOFFICIAL**,
and the big sponsor block is **WORK IS WORK** styled like a telecom/airline mark.
Official-merch energy (crisp whites, metallic gold) anchored in the club's
**safety-cone orange**. Everything that signals "premium official drop" — the foil,
the hospitality-tier gloss, the limited-edition stamp — gets bent into the language
of a cover charge and a VIP rope.

## The faux-sponsor board
Placed exactly where a real kit's commercial inventory sits. Read top to bottom,
it's the night's hierarchy: who gets paid, who gets billed.
- **Front sponsor (chest):** `WORK IS WORK` — set like a carrier/airline wordmark.
- **Sleeve cuff:** `OFFICIAL HOSPITALITY PARTNER` over `PUBLIC LAND / PRIVATE PROFIT`.
- **Back-neck micro-sponsor:** `SAFER SPACES FC`.
- **Shorts tag:** `CITY LOVES THE STORY, NOT THE WORKER`.
- **Alt / protest slot:** `DECRIM NOW`.

## Tech-pack beats
- **Base:** safety-cone orange body, white sleeves, metallic-gold trim.
- **Chest:** vertical "pole" stripe as a sleek metallic gradient (stage-culture nod).
- **Numbers:** built from human silhouettes — tourists queueing vs. staff/dancers
  exiting a back door. Zoom in → the hierarchy.
- **Patch spoof:** where "FIFA Quality Pro" sits → `UNLICENSED / UNPAID / UNAPOLOGETIC`.
- **Trophy misuse:** trophy silhouette → buzzing neon door marquee.
- **Collar:** mock VIP-wristband band print, perforated like a tear-off ticket.
- **Inside hem:** a printed "tab" — itemized cover charge, drink minimum, "service
  fee" — the bill for a night you never agreed to.
- **Swing tag / hangtag:** styled as a door-list clipboard — *"Are you on the list?
  You paid for the building."* — carrying the receipts (below) on the reverse.
- **Care label:** `HANDLE THE WORKER WITH THE CARE YOU GIVE THE BRAND`.
- **Back-neck badge:** `NO GAME WITHOUT THE GROUND`.
