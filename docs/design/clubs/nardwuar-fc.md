# NARDWUAR FC — "Deep Cut Home Kit"

**Hero kit:** HOME · **No.97** (APEC, Vancouver, 1997) · Target: unaccountable power,
the unasked question, sanitized civic PR. Data: `src/data/clubs.js#nardwuar-fc`.

> **TV read:** a fun, punky host-nation home kit. **Street read:** a walking archive
> of Vancouver's underground canon — a jersey that argues research *is* the protest.

## Truth to power
Homage to **Nardwuar the Human Serviette** — hyper-researched guerrilla interviewer,
frontman of The Evaporators, rooted at CiTR/UBC — who has ambushed everyone from
Nirvana to prime ministers with deep local history and receipts (famously the APEC
1997 pepper-spray exchange with PM Chrétien). He **is** the project's thesis:
*research is the protest; the receipt is the weapon.* A World Cup jersey turned into
a walking archive that asks who gets to tell Vancouver's story — and who does the
digging.

Where the host city's broadcast is a sanitized civic PR reel, this kit is the
counter-reel: the venues that got paved, the bands that got priced out, the question
the press conference was built to avoid. It is the methodology of the whole project
worn on the body — *do the homework, then ask power the thing it's dodging.*

## How it steals FIFA 2026
Reads as a **host-nation home kit** — bold primaries, classic striping, crest, World
Cup patch — but the crest art is a collage of 7" records, photocopied flyers, and a
tartan-hat silhouette in place of a lion/maple leaf. The "official broadcast"
accreditation is recoded into an unsanctioned press pass. Turns the global broadcast
into an Easter-egg hunt for the city's underground instead of civic PR.

## The faux-sponsor board
The commercial inventory becomes a press credential and a research method.
- **Sleeve "Fair Play" badge:** `DEEP RESEARCH` over `HUMAN SERVIETTE REPORTING CLUB`.
- **Front sponsor:** `WHO BENEFITS? WHO PAYS?` — set as a broadcaster bug.
- **Hem tag:** `INTERVIEW YOUR CITY LIKE NARDWUAR`.
- **Inside collar:** `DOOT DOOLA DOOT DOO…`.
- **Alt slot:** `KEEP ON ROCKIN' IN THE FREE WORLD`.

## Tech-pack beats
- **Colourway:** tartan-inspired (reds/greens/yellows) ghosted into the fabric as a
  clean check.
- **All-over print:** ultra-faint collage — VHS labels, cassette spines, 7" labels,
  microtype interview quotes + local-history notes (Gastown, punk venues, CiTR).
- **Name/number:** nameplate `NARDWUAR!!` in chaotic cut-and-paste ransom-note type;
  number built from tiny record-sleeve / zine-panel rectangles.
- **Trophy misuse:** trophy silhouette → mic stand / vinyl spindle.
- **Swing tag / hangtag:** a mock press credential — *"PRESS THEY DIDN'T VET"* — with
  a perforated lanyard hole; receipts on the reverse.
- **Care label:** `RESEARCH BEFORE WASH. CITE BEFORE WEAR.`
- **Back-neck badge:** `NO GAME WITHOUT THE GROUND`.

**Affectionate tribute to a living, beloved Vancouverite** — he's the hero, power is
the target. Channel the ethos (research → truth to power); do **not** impersonate his
likeness or commercialize his name. If this goes past exhibition toward merch, **get
Nardwuar's blessing first** (he's active and approachable).
