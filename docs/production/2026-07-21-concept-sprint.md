# Clean-sheet jersey concept sprint

## Brief

Create a durable BC + AI team uniform for trade shows, community events, and future member use. It must read clearly across a room, feel credible after the hackathon, avoid dependence on a living person's identity, and be simple enough to manufacture consistently in a small Indian production run.

Source language was drawn from Kris Krug's existing BC + AI narrative: the community can be curious about the opportunity and critical of the party line at the same time; local, regional, and in-person work matters; membership is both a seat in the room and what keeps the room open.

Nothing visual from the submitted Nardwuar concept was treated as a starting point.

## Criteria

Each direction was scored from 1 to 5 for authentic meaning, distance read, repeat wearability, rights safety, manufacturability, and ability to expand into a coherent kit.

| Direction | Meaning | Read | Wear | Rights | Make | Expand | Total |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| BOTH / AND | 5 | 5 | 5 | 5 | 5 | 4 | 29 |
| THE ROOM | 5 | 4 | 4 | 5 | 4 | 3 | 25 |
| BUILT FROM BELOW | 5 | 4 | 4 | 5 | 4 | 2 | 24 |
| TWELVE EYES | 4 | 3 | 3 | 5 | 3 | 3 | 21 |

## Selected direction: BOTH / AND

The plus sign becomes the system rather than a decorative logo. It holds productive tensions together:

- curious + critical
- local + future
- individual + collective
- technology + public interest

The visual language is a custom square-grid alphabet built from simple vector cells. It has no font licensing dependency and reproduces cleanly in screen print, embroidery, signage, or pixels. The production kit uses a stock dark fabric and two print colours, with no full-body sublimation. This reduces colour drift, cost, and quality-control risk while keeping the object unmistakable from booth distance.

## Directions not carried forward

- `THE ROOM` was warm and community-minded, but the phrase needs context before it becomes legible as a uniform.
- `BUILT FROM BELOW` captured grassroots energy, but reads more like a campaign line than an enduring team identity.
- `TWELVE EYES` had a strong collective-intelligence premise, but risked becoming a mascot or illustration exercise and was harder to manufacture simply.
