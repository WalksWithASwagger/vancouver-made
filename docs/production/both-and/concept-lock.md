# BCAI-01 concept lock

## Design statement

`BOTH / AND` is a team uniform for people building the future while retaining the right to question it. The garment carries a clear BC + AI identifier on the front and the organizing idea on the back. The plus is always an active joint between positions, not a generic tech symbol.

## Locked visual system

- Near-black navy garment body
- Bone primary lettering
- Signal lime and coastal ice as the two active signal colours
- Custom 5 by 7 square-grid lettering only for exterior artwork
- Large front identifier and large back statement
- `LOCAL` and `FUTURE` sleeve markers
- Matching shorts with a small embroidered identifier and printed concept mark
- No crest, tartan, mascot, portrait, player name, number, sponsor, gradient, distress texture, or all-over pattern

## Production intent

The first run prioritizes hand feel, durability, and repeatability over novelty. The preferred fabric is a premium 180-200 gsm recycled polyester birdseye or interlock with moisture management and a soft matte face. Ocean-bound or GRS-certified recycled input is preferred when the mill can document it, but quality and truthful sourcing take precedence over an unsupported claim.

Exterior graphics use polyester-safe low-cure ink with dye-migration blocking. The factory must test the actual fabric and return a strike-off before bulk. The right-short wordmark is a simple two-colour embroidery; the factory must return a sew-out. The inside neck mark may use heat transfer if it is softer and more durable than screen print on the selected fabric.

## Approval rule

No creative direction changes follow this lock. Factory feedback may adjust construction, grading, minimum line weight, thread path, or placement tolerances when required for production. Any such change must be shown in a marked digital proof and approved before the pre-production sample.
