## 1. What this document is

*why* and ChatGPT gives the *system*, Claude gives the **citations** — 24 named
precedent works across 4 threads, each with creator, year, source URL, rights

It is organized as a **reference library**, not a kit concept doc. It doesn't

## 3. The transferable techniques (this is the gold)

The "visual grammar" lessons are directly buildable design tactics:

- **Perfect mimicry, inverted payload** (Brandalism / Yes Men) — reproduce the
  official visual system *exactly*, then twist the message. Doc calls this "the
  most reliably powerful single tactic in the corpus." → our sponsor-parody bank.
- **Coded detail over slogans** (1968) — load socks/beads/scarf/gloves with
  meaning. Subtler, more durable, *harder for a rulebook to police*. → number
  logic, trim, collar details instead of just big text.
- **Layer the counter-message OVER the official jersey** (Norway tees) — literally
  overwrite the brand surface. → a wearable, on-pitch-legible concept.
- **Design the kit as a future artifact** (Smith's tracksuit at NMAAHC) → museum-grade framing for the deck.
- **The deliberate empty slot** (Rigo 23 statue, Norman's empty spot) → designed
  participation; leave a blank the wearer completes.
- **"World Cup of ___" counter-lockup** (Amnesty) → ready-made counter-brand format.
- **Put the prohibiting rule text on the kit** (Rule 50 / FIFA code) → the rulebook
  policing the uniform IS the concept. Print the ban.
- **Recode the sport's central object** (Paulo Ito's ball-on-a-plate) → the football
  itself as the indictment.

## 4. The two anchors Doc 03 insists on (both Vancouver-local)

1. **Cowichan sweater (2010)** — named "the project's single closest precedent."
   HBC sold a $350 knockoff "Olympic sweater" instead of authentic Coast Salish
   hand-knits; after protest, HBC agreed to sell genuine sweaters (on poor terms).
   This is *the* knockoff-vs-authentic story — exactly the kit project's theme.
2. **Adbusters** — founded **Vancouver, 1989** (Kalle Lasn + Bill Schmalz).
   Subvertising / détournement / Buy Nothing Day / the Occupy call. **Vancouver's
   homegrown protest-design heritage** — the project can claim direct local lineage.

## 7. Where Doc 03 lands on the risk-appetite fork

Doc 03 is **closer to ChatGPT than Perplexity** on caution, but for a *craft*
reason, not a scolding one: as a **settler artist**, center Indigenous design
sovereignty — **cite, don't reproduce**. Its hard line is specifically about
**reproducing protected Indigenous designs or copyrighted agency photos in
sellable merch** — that's the "stop and license" threshold. It is otherwise fully
on board with aggressive brand-hijack against FIFA/sponsors/the city.

→ Useful reframe for the owner (who wants maximum edge): **the edge gets sharper,
not duller, when aimed through real precedent.** "Perfect mimicry + inverted
payload" is more savage than generic satire because it's legally and visually
exact. The only genuine restraint Doc 03 argues for is not appropriating Coast
Salish/Indigenous form — which all three docs independently agree on.
