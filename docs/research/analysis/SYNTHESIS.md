## 1. The thesis the three docs converge on

> **Most kits turn a city into a logo. This one turns it into evidence.**
> A counter-World-Cup football collection that borrows the tournament's official
> visual language — kits, crests, sponsor bars, fan-zone branding — and reissues it
> as a *receipt* for what Vancouver pays and who gets erased. Built from primary
> sources. Every garment has a paper trail. **Not anti-football. Anti-amnesia.**

All three agree on the core move (uniform as message surface / brandalism),
the local grounding (named Vancouver histories beat generic "FIFA bad"), and
the process-as-deliverable angle (a reproducible pipeline wins "novelty of process").

## 2. Naming / architecture (recommendation)

- **Umbrella label:** keep **VANCOUVER MADE** (repo, domain, the collective voice)
  with **"Host City / Ghost City"** as the tagline/exhibition title.
- **Structure:** a **league of fake clubs** (ChatGPT's frame), each indicting one
  system. This is the richest, most production-ready container.
- **Voice:** edgy, provocative, in-your-face (owner's call) — but sharpened through
  *real precedent*. "Perfect mimicry + inverted payload" (Doc 03) is more savage
  than loose satire because it's exact.

## 4. The design system (league logic — from ChatGPT, armed by Claude)

- **One shield geometry** + one "evidence cut" line across all crests.
- **Two typefaces:** a condensed grotesk (names/numbers) + a monospace (source
  metadata, back-neck text). Sportswear + record-keeping in one visual sentence.
- **"Document edge" trim** on every kit: tear line, docket tab, filing punch,
  permit stamp, or barricade stripe. Politics structural, not decorative.
- **Raw material = bureaucracy:** receipts, redactions, maps, permits, by-laws,
  dockets, accreditation badges, invoices.
- **Sponsor-parody, never logo-copy:** original typography + commentary language.
  (Legal + ethical: don't reproduce FIFA marks; do eviscerate them in your own voice.)

## 5. The five transferable techniques to actually build (from Doc 03)

1. **Perfect mimicry, inverted payload** — official format, twisted message.
2. **Coded detail over slogans** — meaning in socks/trim/numbers; rulebook-proof.
3. **Counter-message layered OVER the jersey** — Norway-tee move, on-pitch legible.
4. **Print the prohibiting rule** — Rule 50 / FIFA disciplinary text AS the graphic.
5. **Recode the football** — the sport's central object as the indictment.

## 6. The process spine (the hackathon-winning move)

All three docs independently describe the same pipeline. Unified:

```
CIVIC WOUND → PRIMARY SOURCES → HARM MAP (institution / money / community / dates)
→ EXTRACT VISUAL TOKENS → ETHICAL RISK CHECK → CLUB + KIT → BUILD ASSETS
→ QR SOURCE CARD (claim + source + rights) → MOCKUPS → REVIEW → PITCH
```

(Perplexity's "Land→Data→Pattern→Jersey" + ChatGPT's "receipt-to-kit" + Claude's
techniques + rights metadata.) **This is what the R3F portal should visualize and
what the deck should foreground.** The system is itself a deliverable.

## 7. The two local anchors (project legitimacy — from Doc 03)

- **Cowichan sweater (2010)** — the closest precedent in the corpus: knockoff vs.
  authentic, who profits from culture at a mega-event. Lead the "why us, why here."
- **Adbusters (Vancouver, 1989)** — homegrown subvertising heritage. Claim the lineage.

## 8. The one real decision left for the owner: the risk dial

The docs agree on *who to punch up at* (systems, institutions, FIFA, developers,
the city) and on one shared restraint: **don't appropriate Coast Salish/Indigenous
form — cite, don't reproduce.** They diverge only on **how far to push the
trauma-adjacent clubs** (MMIWG / overdose / sex work). Perplexity leans in;
ChatGPT + Claude counsel discipline there.

That dial is the owner's to set, kit by kit. Nothing here forecloses the edge —
it just maps where the edge is sharpest (finance, displacement, security theatre,
greenwashing, graft) vs. where it needs the most care to avoid punching down.
