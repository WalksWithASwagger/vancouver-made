## The thesis (one sentence)

FIFA World Cup 2026 is coming to Vancouver, and VANCOUVER MADE turns the
tournament's own visual language — kits, crests, trophies, fan-zone portals —
back on itself to indict international sports greed and the displacement that
mega-events leave behind.

## Why this lands in Vancouver specifically

Vancouver is a real FIFA World Cup 2026 host city (matches at BC Place). It also
carries a well-documented history of mega-event boosterism colliding with a
housing and affordability crisis. The protest writes itself because the city has
literally run this play before.
