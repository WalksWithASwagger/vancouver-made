# Perplexity — Three-Concept Build-Out (Number 5 · Nardwuar · Pump & Dump)

> Second Perplexity contribution. Specific design build-out for the three flagship
> concepts the owner chose, with explicit FIFA-2026 brand-hijack direction and a
> shared spoof system. Imported verbatim.

Here's how to build out those three concepts so they *feel* like official FIFA 2026 gear while absolutely turning the brand back on itself and asking who benefits / who pays.

***

## Number 5: "Work Is Work Away Strip"

**Truth to power:**
No. 5 Orange is one of Vancouver's most iconic strip clubs, rooted in the old Melbourne Hotel (1904) and turned into No. 5 Orange in 1971, later shifting into a daytime strip club and landmark that's hosted everyone from Wayne Gretzky to Bon Jovi and features heavily in local lore and film. The kit uses that legacy to talk about sex work as labor, the city's hypocrisy about "edgy nightlife," and who carries risk versus who gets the stories.[1][2][3][4]

**How it hijacks FIFA 2026 branding:**
- Use a classic **World Cup away-kit template**: clean paneling, host-city patch on the sleeve, and a trophy silhouette watermark—except your "host city" crest says `VANCOUVER 2026 – UNOFFICIAL`.
- Colour palette riffs on **official merch energy** (crisp whites, metallic gold accents) but anchored in that unmistakable **safety-cone orange** of the club's façade.[2][1]
- Replace a big sponsor logo with bold type: `WORK IS WORK` or `DECRIM NOW`, styled like a telecom or airline mark.

**Key visual beats:**
- **Base colour:** saturated orange body with white sleeves, read instantly as "No. 5" for locals and just "punchy away kit" for outsiders.[1][2]
- **Chest stripe:** a vertical "pole" stripe down the torso, rendered like a sleek metallic gradient in full FIFA fashion photography, but clearly a nod to the club's stage culture.
- **Faux sponsor ecosystem:**
  - Front sponsor: `WORK IS WORK`.
  - Back-of-neck micro sponsor: `SAFER SPACES FC`.
  - Shorts sponsor: tiny tag `CITY LOVES THE STORY, NOT THE WORKER`.
- **Number treatment:** numbers built from tiny silhouettes of people: tourists lining up on one side, staff and dancers exiting a back door on the other—zoom in and you see the hierarchy.
- **Official patch spoof:** where a "FIFA Quality Pro" patch might sit, print `UNLICENSED / UNPAID / UNAPOLOGETIC`.

**Message:**
Looks like a glam World Cup away strip on TV, but anyone who reads it realizes it's a jersey for the people whose labor props up the city's nightlife while policy and stigma leave them exposed.

## Nardwuar!!: "Deep Cut Home Kit"

**Truth to power:**
Nardwuar is a Vancouver institution: a hyper-researched, guerrilla interviewer and frontman of The Evaporators, rooted at CiTR and UBC, who's been ambushing everyone from Nirvana to politicians for decades with deep local history and receipts. This kit is about **journalism, receipts, and memory**—a World Cup jersey turned into a walking archive that asks: who gets to tell Vancouver's story, and who actually does the digging?[5][6][7][8][9]

**How it hijacks FIFA 2026 branding:**
- Visually, it reads like a **host nation home kit**: bold primary colours, classic striping, crest, World Cup patch.
- Crest shape mimics the official shield silhouettes used in host branding, but the crest art is a **collage of records, photocopied show flyers, and a tartan hat silhouette** in place of a lion or maple leaf.[8][9][5]
- Sleeve patch riffs on FIFA's "Fair Play" badges, but yours says `DEEP RESEARCH` and `HUMAN SERVIETTE REPORTING CLUB`.

**Key visual beats:**
- **Colourway:** tartan-inspired palette (reds, greens, yellows) woven into a modern football strip—like a clean check pattern ghosted into the fabric.[9][8]
- **All-over pattern:** ultra-faint collage of:
  - VHS labels, cassette spines, 7" record labels from Vancouver bands.
  - Microtype quotes from legendary interviews and local history notes (Gastown, punk venues, CiTR).
- **Back name & number:**
  - Nameplate: `NARDWUAR!!` in a slightly chaotic, cut-and-paste type.
  - Number formed from tiny rectangles representing record sleeves and zine panels.
- **Hidden details:**
  - Inside collar print: `DOOT DOOLA DOOT DOO...`
  - Hemline tag: `INTERVIEW YOUR CITY LIKE NARDWUAR`.

**Message:**
On the surface: fun, punky Vancouver home kit. Underneath: a jersey for hyper-local, obsessive, DIY journalism—turning the global World Cup broadcast into an Easter-egg hunt for Vancouver's underground canon instead of sanitized civic PR.

***

## Pump & Dump!: "Speculation City Third Kit"

**Truth to power:**
Vancouver's housing market has been driven by years of speculative investment and foreign money inflows, decoupling prices from local incomes and creating a "freak show" of unaffordability. There are thousands of unsold condos and inventories of empty units, while developers and investors capture upside and residents face extreme cost burdens. This kit treats the city like a pump-and-dump ICO or meme coin: early insiders pump, everyone else gets wrecked.[10][11][12][13][14]

**How it hijacks FIFA 2026 branding:**
- Looks like a **limited-edition "city pride" third kit** that every host city releases to cash in on the hype.[15][16][17]
- Uses World Cup trophy silhouettes and 2026 host typography in the background, but replaces the trophy on the chest with a gleaming **glass condo tower** made of stacked coins.
- Sponsor patch mimics a big bank/crypto logo, but the brand is `PUMP & DUMP CAPITAL` with a fake tagline like `WE WIN IF YOU LIVE HERE OR NOT`.

**Key visual beats:**
- **Base colour:** midnight navy or deep black, with toxic-neon gradients (teal, magenta) straight out of hyped-up 2026 promo graphics.[16][18][19]
- **All-over pattern:**
  - Line charts soaring up, then cliff-diving—annotations like `2021 PEAK`, `FLIP TAX`, `FOREIGN BUYER SURCHARGE`.
  - Tiny condo towers as repeating icons; some lit (occupied), most dark (vacant), referencing unsold and investor-owned units.[12][13][14][10]
- **Crest:**
  - Shield split into two: one half a soccer ball, the other half a candlestick chart / condo stack.
  - Motto ribbon: `INFLATE. EXTRACT. ABANDON.`
- **Name & number:**
  - Players replaced with roles: `DEVELOPER`, `FLIPPER`, `LANDLORD`, `RENOVICTOR`.
  - Numbers built from floor-plans and MLS listing codes.
- **Shorts/socks:**
  - Shorts feature a tiny `VACANT` stamp pattern where a sponsor ad would sit.
  - Socks have percentage numbers down the calf (`22x`, `33x`) hinting at price-to-income ratios and absurd multiples.[13][10]

**Message:**
Reads like a sexy, hypebeast-friendly blackout kit on TikTok; actually a wearable indictment of how Vancouver treated housing as a speculative asset while branding itself as a livable, global World Cup city.

***

## Shared FIFA 2026 spoof elements across all three

To make the set feel coherent and very "World Cup, but evil twin":

- **Unified "host city" crest frame:** same shield outline on all three, with different inner art (strip club / record archive / condo stack).
- **Faux tournament patches:** one sleeve patch that looks like a FIFA 2026 mark, but text reads `UNOFFICIAL HOST CITY DROP` and `THIS KIT IS NOT LICENSED BY ANYONE`.
- **Trophy silhouette misuse:** trophy shape used as:
  - Neon marquee for Number 5.
  - Mic stand / vinyl spindle for Nardwuar.
  - Condo tower / speculative bubble for Pump & Dump.[20][21]
- **Campaign line across all:** `NO GAME WITHOUT THE GROUND` as the campaign, with a small badge on the back neck of each kit.

From here, you can pick one of these as the primary kit for today and keep the other two as concept slides / secondary art.
