## Slide 6 — Our Process
"A receipts engine, not a t-shirt"
1. **Mimic the official polish** — rebuilt FIFA & sponsor visual language exactly:
   lockups, sponsor bars, kit-maker spec type.
2. **Invert the payload** — where the brand message goes, the indictment goes. The
   Brandalism move, applied to a kit.
3. **Bake in the receipt** — every claim carries its citation on the hem, in
   kit-maker spec type. The data is the craft.
Mined from public record: BC budget updates, the Oppal inquiry, Olympic housing
data, FIFA cost reports. ("What we printed on the hem — public cost, CAD millions.")
