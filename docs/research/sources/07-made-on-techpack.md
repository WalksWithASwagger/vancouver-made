## The method (the heart of it)
Section 5 "Artwork & Placement" is where the protest lives. **Rule for the
collection: any factual claim on the garment carries its source on the hem in
kit-maker spec type. The receipts are part of the garment, not a caption beside it.**

Placement map: (1) crest/left chest · (2) sponsor bar/centre · (3) maker mark/right ·
(4) neck tape · (5) nameplate · (6) number · (7) lower-back citation · (8) hem citation
(printed where fibre-content/wash-care goes).

## Collection palette
Ink `0E0E0E` · Bone `EDE6D8` · Stamp red `C0392B` · Gold `B8924A`.

## BOM (bill of materials)
- Main fabric: recycled poly performance knit, ~150 gsm, moisture-wicking.
- Collar/cuffs: rib knit, self or contrast.
- Print: dye-sublimation for full-body graphics; fine screen / high-density heat
  transfer for micro-type (hem citations).
- Main label: woven, inside neck. Hangtag: recycled card = the manifesto card.

## Print notes
- Full-body/allover (banknote engraving, ledger lines, ToS text): dye-sublimation
  before cut-and-sew for edge-to-edge coverage.
- Micro-type (hem citations): confirm min legible point size with Formme.
- Redaction bars / matte-on-matte: spec contrasting *finish* (matte body vs gloss
  bar), not just colour.
- Metallic gold (Kit 03): confirm Formme can run metallic; else warm ochre that reads gold.

## Labelling / hangtag / packaging
- Woven label: MADE ON · style no. · size · "made on unceded xʷmǝθkʷǝy̓ǝm,
  Sḵwx̱wú7mesh, sǝlilwǝtaɬ territory."
- Care label: standard wash care + one line of the citation continued.
- Hangtag = manifesto card: MADE ON statement (short form) one side; the kit's
  receipts + a QR to a DTES / land-back org on the other.
- Packaging: minimal, recycled, unbranded — the anti-merch finish.

## Construction
Standard football-jersey construction; no structural changes; flatlock/coverstitch,
tonal thread; woven/heat-seal labels only. "All the design work is on the surface."

## Worked example — KIT 03: MADE ON THE PUBLIC DIME (the banknote kit)
Concept: the World Cup as a public-money transfer with grass on top, worn as legal tender.
Colorway: body = banknote green `1B4D3E`; primary print = engraved gold `B8924A`
(metallic if possible); secondary = ink `0E0E0E`.
Artwork:
- Crest: casino chip with FIFA-shield silhouette.
- Sponsor bar: "BANK OF FIFA" in engraved banknote type.
- Nameplate: "LEGAL TENDER" arched where the player name sits.
- Number: "$729,000,000" denomination.
- Back line: "the house always wins."
- Hem citation: `SECURITY $242M · NET TO BC TAXPAYER UP TO $114M · CALIFORNIA WENT
  PRIVATE · YOU DIDN'T` — source: CBC / Globe and Mail, 2026.
- Allover: guilloché banknote engraving (sublimated). Serial no. on back:
  `SER. NO. VAN-2026-PUBLIC`.
